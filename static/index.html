FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, files, materials, and delivery.

What 3D file formats do you accept?

We accept all standard 3D formats including STL, 3MF, STEP (.step/.stp), and OBJ. For CAD design or reverse-engineering, you can also upload dimensioned PDF drawings, sketches, or photos.

What is your maximum build volume?

Our standard single-piece build volume is 256 × 256 × 256 mm. Larger models can be split with alignment pin joints and assembled seamlessly with structural bonding.

How fast is delivery within Portugal and the EU?

Most orders print in 24–48 hours. Shipping in Portugal takes 24 hours via CTT Express (€4.50, free over €50). European Union deliveries take 2–4 business days via DHL/UPS.

I have never ordered a 3D print before. What should I do?

It's completely painless! Head to our Get a Quote page and drag your file into the box. If you don't have a 3D file, switch to "I need it designed" and write a description. We will guide you step by step.
