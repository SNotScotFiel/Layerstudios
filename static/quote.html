Quick answers

What formats do you accept?

STL, 3MF, STEP (.step/.stp), OBJ, and ZIP archives. For design requests, we also accept PDF drawings, photos, and sketches.

How long does it take?

Most orders are printed and shipped within 24–48 hours after payment. Complex or large parts may take 3–5 business days. We'll confirm the timeline when we review your quote.

What's the maximum part size?

Our build volume is 256 × 256 × 256 mm. Larger parts can be split and glued together — just mention it in the notes.

I don't have a 3D file — can you still help?

Absolutely. Switch to "I need it designed" above, describe what you need, and optionally upload a photo, sketch, or PDF. Our team will create the 3D model and share it for your approval.
